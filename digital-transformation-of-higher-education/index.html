DDigital Transformation of Higher Education

Exploratory inquiry · v0.9.0 · 31 October 2026

Copies multiplied. Distance shrank. Search became instant. Now reasoning is being delegated to machines. What could universities be for, and who decides?

Questions at three levels

Peoplemicro

Lectures reached the kitchen table, and literature could be found in seconds. Now drafts, analyses and answers can be generated. What happens to the everyday work of teaching, researching and learning, and who benefits?

Institutionsmeso

Correspondence, broadcast and the web extended the campus, while journals, indexes and platforms took on evaluation and publishing around it. What happens to degrees, research organisation and governance once reasoning is delegated to machines?

Societymacro

Academies, publishers, libraries and platforms each carry part of the work of knowing. If reasoning is delegated to machines, what place does the university have in the knowledge system, and what mandate justifies it?

Evidence and method

How to read this inquiry

Of higher education, not only in it

The inquiry concerns changes to institutional form, functions, boundaries, legitimacy and mandate, not only whether tools improve learning inside existing structures.

Evidence, interpretation and speculation stay apart

Observed or documented material, interpretation, debated claims, expectations and projections are labelled separately. Historical cases are analogies, not forecasts.

Runs of different size are not interchangeable.

The same question asked in a run that included about 20 papers and in a run that included about 100 papers returned largely different sets of papers. The three larger runs on the meso, macro and cross-level questions cited 198 distinct papers, and only 9, 9 and 14 of them also appear in the smaller runs. Conclusions that rest on a single smaller run are therefore treated as scoping.

AI-assisted and unverified

The literature syntheses were produced with an AI literature-search tool; extraction, coding and draft copy were produced by an AI model. Nothing has been verified against the underlying papers.

Publication boundary

The public site uses paraphrased curated data and bibliographic facts. Verbatim tool outputs, prompts, abstracts, extracted passages, private links and internal processing records are not published.

Out of scope

No forecasts, country or region rankings, stage models, composite rate-of-change index, unsupported causal claims, or doctoral-training analysis.
