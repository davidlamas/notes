Evidence and method

How to read this inquiry

Of higher education, not only in it

The inquiry concerns changes to institutional form, functions, boundaries, legitimacy and mandate—not only whether tools improve learning inside existing structures.

Evidence, interpretation and speculation stay apart

Observed or documented material, interpretation, debated claims, expectations and projections are labelled separately. Historical cases are analogies, not forecasts.

Pro and Deep are not interchangeable

Pro runs read about 20 abstracts; Deep runs read about 100. Repeated prompts returned substantially different paper sets, so Pro-only material is treated as scoping.

AI-assisted and unverified

Literature syntheses were produced with Consensus. Extraction, coding, synthesis and draft copy were AI-assisted. Most evidence comes from abstracts, and the findings have not yet been verified against the underlying papers.

Publication boundary

The public site uses paraphrased curated data and bibliographic facts. Verbatim tool outputs, prompts, abstracts, snippets, private links and agent traces are not published.

Out of scope

No forecasts, country or region rankings, stage models, composite rate-of-change index, unsupported causal claims, or doctoral-training analysis.
