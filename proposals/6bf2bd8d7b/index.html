Sources and method

Where each item comes from

Every item shows its origin in the detail panel. There are four kinds.

  • Sealed 2026 proposal. Work packages, tasks, deliverables, milestones, objectives, Part B sections and the 2026 budget, read from the submitted proposal (SEP-211281191) and its workbooks.
  • EACEA evaluation. The 39 reviewer observations (19 strengths, 20 weaknesses and risks) and seven root causes, parsed from the ESR response matrix. Scores: Relevance 25 of 30, Design 25 of 30, Partnership 15 of 20, Impact 15 of 20, total 80 of 100.
  • Decided by David. The seven decisions in the pre-blueprint decision log.
  • Proposed by the assistant. The keep, revise and new labels, the fourteen new tasks, the proposed 2027 leads, and the links between reviewer comments and responses. These are proposals until partners confirm them.

Limits

  • The application accepts only Part A, Part B and the budget. Documents to secure are for our own assurance, and each must become a statement in Part B.
  • Task time windows exist only in the graphic timetable of the 2026 proposal, so tasks are not drawn on the timeline. Links from tasks to deliverables were inferred from titles.
  • No 2027 budget or person-month figures are shown. The 2027 call rules and deadline are not verified.
  • Only the TLU–UEM MoU and its addendum are signed instruments. Other partner roles are proposals.
  • The 2026 proposal has inconsistencies we have kept as they are: work-package tables add to 159 person-months against 156 elsewhere, and milestone numbering skips MS3 and repeats MS6 and MS12.
