## Ecology coverage

The interactive ecology includes CHI, AfriCHI, PDC, IHC, OzCHI, NordiCHI, INTERACT, EICS, UIST, ETRA, CSCW, GROUP, DIS, C&C, TEI, HRI, IUI, IDC, COMPASS, and UbiComp. Neighbour links retain additional discovery paths such as Collective Intelligence, SCF, CHI PLAY, CUI, RecSys, UMAP, EduCHI, CritiCo, MobileHCI, AHs, ICMI, AutoUI, and Interactive Health.

The paper set is intentionally smaller than the ecology. Each included paper has a verified bibliographic record and an explicit account of purpose, evidence, contribution, venue relevance, and cross-connections.
