The featured-paper set spans twelve conference communities (CHI, IUI, ETRA, EICS, CSCW, UbiComp, HRI, DIS, Critical Computing, IDC, INTERACT, NordiCHI) plus TOCHI. The ecology is broader than the paper set because inclusion requires individual verification, not quota filling.
