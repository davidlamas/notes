Methodology

How this map was constructed.

The map is a teaching synthesis, not a bibliometric classification or a claim that HCI divides into sealed schools.

01

Begin with warrants

Families were distinguished by what they try to know, what counts as evidence, the contribution they make, and the standards that make a claim credible.

02

Ground methods carefully

Lazar, Feng, and Hochheiser (2017), Olson and Kellogg (2014), and Adams, Lunt, and Cairns (2008) ground research contributions, methodological plurality, evidence, qualitative inquiry, participants, and ethics. No single source provides the whole taxonomy.

03

Read beyond method labels

Turner (2016), Chignell et al. (2023), and the historical overlays clarify cognition, human-technology relations, disciplinary histories, research through design, power, participation, and relational commitments.

04

Validate examples individually

Papers were selected through official proceedings and DOI records. Conference groupings guided discovery; they did not determine placement.

Known limits. The collection now includes work from AfriCHI, IHC, and OzCHI, but it still underrepresents scholarship in languages other than English, Indigenous and decolonial inquiry, accessibility venues beyond this selection, formal methods, replication, and industry-only research.
