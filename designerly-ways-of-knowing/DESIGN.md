# Content and visual inventory

## Central argument

Generative design research creates designed conditions in which people can externalise, negotiate, and transform forms of knowledge that conventional inquiry may struggle to reach. It is not a collection of creative workshop exercises. Framing, materials, facilitation, recruitment, time, consent, analysis, and interpretation all shape what becomes expressible and what claims can be supported.

## Minimum presentation sequence

1. Design is a mode of inquiry.
2. Problems and possible responses develop together.
3. Situations talk back through action and material consequences.
4. People know more than they can readily say.
5. Saying, doing, and making offer different routes to expression.
6. Individual expression can become collective inquiry without implying consensus.
7. Sensitising, sensemaking, provotyping, and futuring stage one possible process.
8. Artefacts do not become findings without interpretation.
9. Democracy Labs show how conditions shape what becomes knowable.
10. The knowledge produced is situated, provisional, and open to revision.

## Exploration structure

- **Map:** framing, action, material engagement, expression, collective interpretation, and provisional claims.
- **Knowledge:** explicit, observable, tacit, and latent knowledge, with a caution against treating depth as direct access to truth.
- **Expression:** say, do, and make across memories, present experience, and possible futures.
- **Process:** sensitising, sensemaking, provotyping, and futuring as a situated sequence rather than a formula.
- **Conditions:** prompts, materials, facilitation, time, recruitment, consent, group dynamics, institutional setting, and analysis.
- **Case:** Democracy Labs as designed epistemic infrastructure, including minority criticism and limits.
- **Teaching:** the assignment sequence as an optional path from framing to fieldwork.
- **Sources and limits:** conceptual, methodological, applied, and course sources kept distinct.

## Visual decisions

### Preserve or closely adapt

- The calm pastel palette and overlapping fields used across the course decks.
- The design-research landscape as a simplified orientation map.
- The layered path of expression from memories through the present to possible futures.
- The research-to-design bridge from material and interpretation to design possibilities.
- The staged generative process as a compact sequence.

### Reconstruct interactively

- Explicit, observable, tacit, and latent knowledge as a selectable depth navigator.
- Say, do, and make as a comparison controlled by selection.
- The path of expression as a temporal selector.
- Sensitising, sensemaking, provotyping, and futuring as a process navigator.
- Designed conditions as selectable influences on expression and interpretation.
- Analysis as movement from traces to patterns, insights, imaginaries, and possibilities.

### Redraw simply

- Problem and response co-evolution.
- Reflection in and on action.
- Individual expression to collective interpretation.
- Research material to design possibility.

### Deliberately omit

- Repeated full-process slides duplicated across two decks.
- Dense planning and analysis tables that do not serve the central argument.
- A general history of creativity research.
- An encyclopaedic catalogue of tools and methods.
- Workshop photographs and identifiable participant material.
- Consent forms, contact details, private links, and local source documents.

## Claims requiring qualification

- Tacit and latent knowledge are interpretations, not objects extracted intact.
- Making can support expression, but does not automatically reveal truth.
- Artefacts and prompts shape what can be expressed.
- Collective artefacts do not establish consensus.
- Positive participant evaluations do not prove effectiveness.
- Recruitment, facilitation, confidence, education, expertise, and power affect participation.
- Future imaginaries remain shaped by familiar institutions and repertoires.
- Democracy Labs demonstrate possibilities and tensions, not a universal method.

## Public material boundary

Only purpose-made HTML, CSS, JavaScript, compact paraphrases, and verified public references will be published. No source PDF, PPTX, DOCX, participant image, participant quotation with identifying detail, personal information, or private research link will be copied into the repository.
