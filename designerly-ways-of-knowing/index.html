Designerly Ways of Knowing

How can proposing, making and reflecting help people understand a situation while trying to change it? Explore design as inquiry, generative research as an arranged encounter, and Democracy Labs as a case with visible evidence and limits.

The inquiry uses a bounded set of inspected records. An unverified link is not proof that nothing happened beyond those records.
