Generative research

Research that creates conditions for something not yet fully formed to emerge.

The phrase can describe inquiries that generate ideas, accounts, hypotheses, or futures. Its meaning depends on the field and should not be assumed from the adjective alone.

Generative design research

A participatory design-research tradition centred on expression through making.

Here it refers more specifically to research that combines sensitising, saying, doing, making, collective creativity, and future-oriented exploration so that tacit or latent knowing can take provisional form.
