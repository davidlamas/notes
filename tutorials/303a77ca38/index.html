About this page

What this is, and what it is not

  • A working script and a place to explore, for the evaluation project of the course. It is not a replacement for the theory covered in class, and Moodle is the source of truth.
  • The fifteen steps and the lab numbers come from the course page on Moodle. The minutes inside each lab are suggestions. Each session is 135 minutes (three academic hours). No dates are shown.
  • Notes for teachers and answers are visible to students. That is on purpose.
  • The page is not linked from anywhere and is marked noindex, but the host is public. It holds no personal data, no accounts and no study data.
  • Your choices are not saved. A link keeps the view you are on.
