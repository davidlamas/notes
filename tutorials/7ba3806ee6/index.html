About this page

What this is, and what it is not

  • A working script and a place to explore, for a lab on formative evaluation. It is not a replacement for the theory covered in class.
  • The scenario is invented. The Metro do Porto website is real and public.
  • Notes for teachers and answers are visible to students. That is on purpose.
  • The page is not linked from anywhere and is marked noindex, but the host is public. It holds no personal data, no accounts and no Loop11 data.
  • Your choices are not saved. A link keeps the view you are on.
